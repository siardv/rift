# Rift — Privacy Policy

**Rift does not collect data. None.**

Rift compares two texts you provide. Comparison happens entirely on your device and works offline. Rift does not send your inputs or comparison results to the developer. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, third-party SDKs, or tracking.

The intended App Store privacy declaration is **"Data Not Collected"**.

Rift saves viewer preferences, such as appearance and text size, locally using Apple's standard preferences storage. Text inputs and comparison results are held in memory; Rift does not maintain a saved comparison history.

You can explicitly export or share a comparison through the iOS share sheet. Those exports go to the location, app, or recipient you choose and may leave your device, for example when you choose cloud storage or a messaging app. The receiving service's privacy policy applies to its handling of that export. Importing from Files may also involve a file provider you choose.

The About screen includes links to this policy and the source repository. Opening a link uses your browser and the destination website's privacy practices.

Rift is open source under the MIT license, so this claim is verifiable in the code: https://github.com/siardv/rift

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/siardv/rift/issues

*Updated 6 October 2026 for Rift 0.1.0. Because the developer collects no app data, there is no developer-held app data to opt out of, delete, or request a copy of. Files you export remain under your control or the control of the receiving service.*

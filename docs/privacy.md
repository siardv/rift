# Rift — Privacy Policy

**Rift does not collect data. None.**

Rift compares two texts you provide. That comparison happens entirely on your device. The app contains no networking code of any kind: nothing is uploaded, downloaded, synced, or logged. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking. Your texts never leave your device.

Accordingly, Rift's App Store privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"** across the board.

The only files Rift ever writes are exports you explicitly create through the iOS share sheet, saved where you choose.

Rift is open source under the MIT license, so this claim is verifiable in the code: https://github.com/siardv/rift

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/siardv/rift/issues

*This policy applies to all versions of Rift. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to opt out of, delete, or request a copy of.*
